## Brand read / two genuinely different directions
Design Read: a familiar Ballynure antique dealer, red-walled rooms, dark wood and silver, direct human contact rather than a distant auction house.
Register: local dealer / object-led editorial; SOUL 8, SPECTACLE 2, DENSITY 5. Taste dials VARIANCE 6, MOTION 1, DENSITY 5.
A SELECTED: seller-first asymmetrical burgundy hero, a single cabinet still life; cool silver-grey ground, broad image-led category passages, quiet attributed quote, real shop/location close. Distinct full-width and split arrangements, no repeated cards.
B REJECTED: pale catalogue gallery with inventory-style tiles, buyer navigation and dealer biography lower down. Different information hierarchy and composition but duplicates Atlas buyer strength and misrepresents generated art as stock too easily.
Reject lazy antique defaults: sepia parchment, ornate crests, beige/brass luxury, script fonts, auction-style numbered lots. Burgundy is justified by actual showroom red walls; silver-grey supplies contrast, no generic gold UI. Soul is honest object patina + direct Hector route. Premium substrate: restrained fine rules, substantial space, crisp system sans plus Georgia display, tactile images. No texture overlays/gradient decoration or gratuitous motion.
